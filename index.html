Why Choose a Career in Beauty in Chennai?

India’s beauty and personal care industry is booming, with a projected revenue of US$31.51 billion by 2024. The personal care segment alone is set to reach US$14.31 billion (Source: Statista). Choosing a career in beauty and personal care in Chennai or any Indian city can be immensely rewarding.

The industry’s continuous expansion, alongside increasing awareness of personal grooming, offers promising career growth opportunities and financial stability.
